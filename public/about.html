Roney Lima do Nascimento

Generative AI for teachers, built by someone who still teaches.

Roney Lima do Nascimento is the author of Generative AI for Teachers and Inteligência Artificial Generativa para Professores; a mathematician and PhD candidate in Pure Mathematics at the University of São Paulo (IME-USP); an independent AI solutions developer and consultant, building AI agents for companies and running AI training for schools and organisations; an IB Diploma Programme Mathematics teacher at Colégio São Luís, São Paulo; and an applied AI researcher whose platforms for education reach more than 15,000 educators every month. Keynote speaker at ICAILY 2026 in Cape Town.
